The legacy SQ formula is normalized asymmetry, not a duration ratio:
(decontacting_duration - contacting_duration) / contact_duration.
CQ and SQ missing masks are independent. ROI CQ and ROI events deliberately
retain distinct filtering/padding rules pending a separately reviewed correction.

long-file filters also use SOS while retaining distinct legacy padding rules. Long-file
edges and pitch outlier references are not whole-recording bitwise equivalents.
